# Junior Data Analyst - Example Ltd, Cambridge
Example Ltd is hiring a Junior Data Analyst to join its commercial insights team in Cambridge. The role is hybrid, with two days per week in the office.
Responsibilities include building Power BI dashboards for the sales team, writing SQL queries against the company data warehouse, cleaning and validating data in Python (pandas), and presenting monthly KPI reports to managers.
Required skills: SQL, Python, Excel and one BI tool such as Power BI or Tableau. A degree in a quantitative subject is required. Experience with statistics and A/B testing is desirable.
Salary range: 30,000 to 35,000 GBP. Visa sponsorship is available for this role. Closing date: 31 October 2026.
